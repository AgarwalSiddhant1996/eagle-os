# Evidence-to-card owner contract

Read only the assigned workspace/item and its authorized evidence. Identify the actual user choice. Engineering tracking or missing source access is not automatically a decision for the user.

Prepare concrete options, a recommendation, why this user decides and what follows their choice. Cite an existing verified evidence id, use a stable deduplication key and the current revision, then record the complete card through the supported local command or host action. Reread the workspace and confirm the card is open in Today.

If evidence is blocked/unverified, state that boundary and request the missing fact instead of inventing a ready card. Do not bypass a denial through another provider.

No external send/write follows from card creation or selecting an option. A later external action needs a separately implemented approval-bound executor and verified authority.

Before a planned pause, record completed work/proof, pending work/owner and the first next step in the authorized local task state. Resume from that state without replaying finished actions unless evidence is missing or the actual result changed.
